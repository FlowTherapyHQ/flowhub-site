Helpful Information

FlowPass FAQ

Quick answers to the questions prospective members ask most often.

What is included in my monthly membership?

FlowPass includes one Full Body Flow massage each month at the duration selected when you enroll, plus the member benefits described on this page.

Is there a minimum membership commitment?

Yes. FlowPass begins with an initial three-month membership commitment. After completing that commitment, membership continues month-to-month.

Can I transfer my session to someone else?

No. FlowPass memberships and monthly membership sessions are nontransferable.

What happens if I cannot use my session?

One unused monthly session may roll into the immediately following month. Unlimited session accumulation is not permitted.

Can members purchase additional massages?

Yes. Members may purchase additional Full Body Flow sessions matching their membership duration and receive $25 off the standard rate.

Which FlowBoosts are available for $15?

Eligible FlowBoosts receive member pricing of $15. Crown Flow and Time Boost are excluded from this benefit.

How do I cancel my membership?

After completing the initial three-month commitment, FlowPass continues month-to-month and requires 30 days’ advance cancellation notice.
